# Microbial Ultrastructure Attribution & Scientific Sources

The **Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 Ultrastructure Atlas** is modeled from experimentally determined structural biology datasets, cryo-electron tomography reconstructions, and curated metabolic/genomic databases:

## Primary Databases & References

1. **EcoCyc Database**:
   - Website: https://ecocyc.org/
   - Reference: Keseler et al. (2021), *The EcoCyc Database: reflecting new knowledge on Escherichia coli K-12*. Nucleic Acids Research, 49(D1), D630–D641.
   - Used for biochemical pathways, protein subunit stoichiometries, and envelope architectures.

2. **Atlas of Bacterial Cell Structure (Caltech Jensen Lab)**:
   - Website: https://www.cellstructureatlas.org/
   - Reference: Briegel et al. (2009), *Bacterial chemoreceptor arrays are organized in a universal architecture*. PNAS.
   - Used for cellular dimensions (~1.0 × 2.5 µm), periplasmic spacing (20–40 nm), and flagellar motor basal body dimensions.

3. **RCSB PDB-101 Mesoscale Landscapes**:
   - Reference: David S. Goodsell (Scripps Research / RCSB PDB), *Cellular Landscapes: Escherichia coli*.
   - Website: https://pdb101.rcsb.org/motm/david-goodsell
   - Used for cytoplasmic macromolecular crowding conventions, 70S ribosome density, and polysome distribution.

4. **Gene Ontology (GO) Consortium**:
   - Standardized biological component terms and cellular compartment accessions (`GO:0009279`, `GO:0019867`, `GO:0042597`, `GO:0009274`, `GO:0005886`, `GO:0009295`, etc.).
